What I do · 研究方向 · 01 · Language models大语言模型

LLMs that read the literature读懂文献的大语言模型

I build LLM pipelines that extract relationships from biomedical papers into knowledge graphs, then check each claim against the data so explanations trace back to evidence.

我构建大语言模型流程，从生物医学文献中抽取关系并构建知识图谱，再用数据逐条核验，让模型的解释可以追溯到证据。

What I do · 研究方向 · 02 · Generative AI and privacy生成式 AI 与隐私计算

Private generative transformers隐私保护的生成式 Transformer

AEGIS is a GPT-style model of lifetime disease histories, trained with differential privacy and audited with planted canary patients, so it learns the disease, not the people.

AEGIS 是一个学习全生命周期疾病史的 GPT 式生成模型，采用差分隐私（DP-SGD）训练，并通过植入“金丝雀”患者进行隐私审计：它学习的是疾病规律，而不是具体的人。

What I do · 研究方向 · 03 · Multimodal AI多模态人工智能

Multimodal AI for multi-omics面向多组学的多模态人工智能

I train models that combine blood proteins, metabolites, genetics and brain MRI from UK Biobank. Depression is the test case: the models predict each of its nine symptoms separately, and every gain is tested against shuffled outcomes.

我基于 UK Biobank 的血液蛋白质组、代谢组、遗传与脑影像数据训练多模态模型。以抑郁症为应用场景：模型分别预测九项症状，并用置换检验验证每一项提升。

    About关于我

    I am a master's student in computer science at Northwestern Polytechnical University in Xi'an, working with Prof. Zhaowen Liu. I came to biomedical AI from software engineering, and I build my research end to end: from data pipelines on UK Biobank to transformers and language models trained on GPU servers.

    我是西北工业大学计算机科学专业的硕士研究生，导师为 刘兆文 教授。我从软件工程转向生物医学人工智能，并端到端地完成自己的研究：从 UK Biobank 数据处理流程，到在 GPU 服务器上训练 Transformer 和大语言模型。

    What connects my projects is a concern for doing this honestly: models that respect how small biological signals are, protect the people in the data, and explain themselves in terms a clinician can check.

    贯穿我所有项目的，是对严谨与诚实的坚持：模型要尊重生物信号的微弱，要保护数据中的每一个人，并能用临床医生可以核验的方式解释自己。
